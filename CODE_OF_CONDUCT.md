# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the ReplayLinkerRP community welcoming, safe, and respectful
for everyone participating in good faith.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

1. Be respectful and constructive.
2. Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
3. Respect project boundaries and community rules.
4. Credit sources and third-party work appropriately.
5. Take responsibility for their contributions and conduct.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, doxxing or disclosure of private information, impersonation, repeated
unwanted contact, and other conduct that threatens the safety or well-being of
the community.

## Reporting

Report suspected violations privately to a repository maintainer or collaborator.
Do not post personal or sensitive details publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may investigate reports and take proportionate action, including a
warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.
